## 2. Copywriting & Content Matrix

### Hero Section
- **Headline:** Beyond Marks. Towards Mastery.
- **Sub-headline:** An evidence-driven academic intelligence platform that connects curriculum understanding, assessment performance, learning mastery, early risk prediction, personalized academic support, and career readiness — all within one unified ecosystem.
- **Key Message:** Academic records should be an engine for student growth, not merely a record of historical grades.

### Problem Section
1. **Fragmented Academic Records:** Attendance, internal quizzes, and lab submissions live in disconnected silos.
2. **Disconnected Curriculum:** Syllabi define course outcomes, but exam marks fail to show which specific outcomes were achieved.
3. **Marks Obscure Conceptual Gaps:** A score of 65% conceals whether a student understands core recursion or failed database transactions.
4. **Late Risk Detection:** Traditional systems flag students only after they fail end-semester examinations.
5. **Untracked Academic Support:** Remediations are verbal, ad-hoc, and rarely monitored for actual learning recovery.
6. **Academic-to-Industry Mismatch:** High GPAs do not guarantee students hold the competencies demanded by modern technical roles.

### Seven Modules Showcase
Each card presents:
- **Module Number & Title**
- **Core Problem Solved**
- **Key Capabilities**
- **Data Ingested (Inputs)**
- **Intelligence Produced (Outputs)**
- **Connection to Subsequent Stage**
- **Direct Interactive Link** to the module in the application demo shell.
